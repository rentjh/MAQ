# 신차 장기렌트 1인 에이전시: 사업 구조, 단위경제, 규제 (as of 2026-10-13)

Research note. About 15 searches and fetches were run on 2026-10-13. Public, citable data on agency commission rates, clawback terms and funnel conversion rates is **very sparse**. These terms sit in private agency contracts (위탁계약서) and closed 영업인 communities such as Naver cafés and KakaoTalk open chats, which search tools cannot index. Labels used below:
- **[검증됨]**: from a primary or official source.
- **[저신뢰]**: from blogs, wikis, vendor promotions or community posts.
- **[배경지식·미검증]**: from the researcher's prior knowledge of statute, not re-fetched in this session. The report writer should present these as items to verify on law.go.kr.

## 1. 대리점/에이전시/카매니저 structure, commissions, clawback, payout; 렌트 vs 리스 registration

### Takeaway
The channel is a chain: lead/상담원 → agent/대리점 → 렌트사·캐피탈. The rental or capital company pays the agent a per-contract "back margin" (백마진/후취 수수료) that is priced into the customer's monthly fee. Public sources give only rough figures: "tens of 만원 per contract", or "1–2% of vehicle price". No public source documents clawback (환수) schedules. Lease (리스) solicitation requires registration as a 금소법 대리·중개업자 (리스·할부 모집인) via 여신금융협회. Long-term rental (렌트) products are not covered by 금소법, so pure rent brokering has no equivalent registration. This is a regulatory gap that the press describes as "비대칭 규제".

### Cited Findings
- **Channel structure:** 상담원 → 에이전트 or 대리점 → 금융사, with "fees and margins added at each step". An unnamed industry source claims intermediaries can add about ₩10M on top of the base vehicle cost. The article covers 나우카, which claims direct codes with 25 financial companies to cut out intermediaries. Article dated 2025-06-01. [저신뢰 for the ₩10M figure] — [넥스트데일리](https://www.nextdaily.co.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=238678)
- **How the dealer is paid:** dealers/brokers receive a per-contract commission from the rental company, called "백마진" or "후취 수수료". It is embedded in the customer's monthly payment, and a higher monthly payment means higher dealer income. The commission is paid "계약 성사 후" (after contract conclusion). The article's illustration: a ₩20–30K/month markup equals ₩0.96–1.44M over 48 months. Article dated 2026-09-01. [저신뢰, consumer-side blog] — [닥터렌트](https://www.dr-rent.net/posts/dealer-fee-zero-truth-car-rental)
- **Commission size:** dealers take "건당 몇 십만원" (tens of 만원 per deal). Namu Wiki also mentions 차량가의 1~2%, with an unclear basis. Namu Wiki also says rental companies earn about a 5–10% financial margin using tax and insurance advantages. [저신뢰, wiki; namu.wiki returned 403, so this is a search snippet only] — [나무위키 장기렌트](https://namu.wiki/w/%EC%9E%A5%EA%B8%B0%EB%A0%8C%ED%8A%B8), [나무위키 신차장기렌트](https://namu.wiki/w/%EC%8B%A0%EC%B0%A8%EC%9E%A5%EA%B8%B0%EB%A0%8C%ED%8A%B8)
- **Customer-side fees** (distinct from the agent commission): 등록비·취급수수료 of roughly ₩100–300K at contract. — [닥터렌트 숨겨진 비용 2026](https://www.dr-rent.net/posts/car-long-term-rental-hidden-fees-guide) [저신뢰]
- **Customer early termination:** 중도해지 위약금 = 잔여렌트료 × 해지위약금률. The rate depends on elapsed time since delivery. 승계수수료 is ₩300K plus VAT (하나캐피탈 product page). Agent clawback is typically pegged to these early-termination and cancellation events, but **no public source confirms this**. — [하나원더카](http://hanaonethecar.com/m/capital/longtermRental)
- **Lease registration (verified):** under 금소법, 리스·할부모집인 must register as a "금융상품 대리·중개업자" through FSS and the industry association. — [금융위원회 보도자료](https://www.fsc.go.kr/po010101/77178)
- **Lease registration process via 여신금융협회 (verified):** complete 리스·할부 교육; new recruiters must pass the 평가; then apply. The fee is ₩20K for an individual and ₩200K for a corporation. 오토론 recruiters register under the same 리스·할부 category (감독규정 제22조제1호바목). — [여신금융협회 모집인 FAQ](https://creditloanagent.crefia.or.kr/faq), [등록시스템](https://loanagent.crefia.or.kr/login)
- **Rental is outside 금소법 (verified press report):** rental companies "sell de facto lease products but are not regulated under 금소법". They have no pre-sale explanation duty, which raises mis-selling concerns. Further points from the same article:
  - Rental companies should in principle register as 시설대여업 under 여전법, but non-registration carries no penalty.
  - Acquisition tax is 4% for commercial rental vehicles vs 7% for lease vehicles. The capital industry claims that equal taxation would raise an extra ₩1조 per year.
  - Debt ratios at end-June 2025: 롯데렌탈 383.4%, SK렌터카 575.1%.
  - Affinity Equity Partners owns SK렌터카 (acquired 2024-08) and agreed to buy 롯데렌탈 in 2025-03, pending FTC review.

  Article dated 2025-09-24. — [서울경제](https://www.sedaily.com/article/14122016)

### Inferences
- A solo agency selling **rent only** needs no 여신금융협회 registration. If it also quotes or closes **lease** products from 현대캐피탈, KB캐피탈, 하나캐피탈 and others, it must register as a 리스·할부 모집인. Registration is per-capital-company, through a contract with the 직접판매업자. Selling lease unregistered breaches 금소법 (unregistered 대리·중개). Many capital companies sell both 렌트 and 리스, so the agent should confirm which product each code covers.
- The commission chain reported here (상담원 → agent → 대리점 → 금융사) implies that a solo agent working under a 대리점's code earns less than one holding a direct company code. This is consistent with the 나우카 "direct codes" pitch.
- Working planning assumption, **unverified**: a per-deal gross commission in the low hundreds of thousands to ~₩1–2M for a domestic mid-size car, i.e. ~1–2% of a ₩35–50M car. The user must verify this against actual 수수료표 from their contracted 대리점.

### Gaps
- No public source gives commission tables for 롯데렌탈, SK렌터카, 현대캐피탈, KB캐피탈 or 하나캐피탈. Nothing public covers per-term, per-vehicle or 프로모션 add-ons either.
- **Clawback (환수):** no public documentation found of the period (e.g. within N months of delivery), the percentage, or the triggers (중도해지, 승계, 연체). Ask the 대리점 for its 위탁계약서.
- Payout timing beyond "after contract conclusion": no source says whether payment follows delivery (출고) or the first payment, or whether payout is monthly.
- No public 렌트사 대리점 모집 공고 with terms was found.

## 2. Funnel benchmarks and purchased DB economics

### Takeaway
No reliable public benchmarks exist for lead → 상담 연결 → 견적 → 계약 conversion in 장기렌트. The only number found is a vendor's self-reported **상담 연결률 of 9–13%** for 저신용 렌트 DB (2025), which is unverified. Public DB vendors mostly advertise CPA/real-time delivery without price lists. The user's ₩38,500 per DB (likely ₩35,000 + VAT) could not be benchmarked against a published auto-rent DB price.

### Cited Findings
- A vendor of 중고차 and 저신용 렌트카 DB (on 아이보스) advertises performance-based pricing rather than per-DB pricing. It claims a 2025 상담 연결률 of 9–13% and says duplicate and old lists are excluded. It states no replacement or refund policy. [저신뢰, vendor ad] — [아이보스 홍보글](https://www.i-boss.co.kr/ab-2987-424149)
- db-store.kr sells 장기렌트/리스 CPA leads in real time via KakaoTalk, email and Excel. No price or duplicate policy is shown. — [db-store.kr](https://db-store.kr/notice/40)
- **Price reference from another industry:** a marketer reports apartment 분양 DB at ₩65,000–85,000 per DB. This is not auto. [저신뢰] — [아이보스 Q&A](https://www.i-boss.co.kr/ab-2110-29519)
- **Unverified contract-term mix:** 24개월 11%, 36개월 35%, 48개월 33%, 60개월 21%. [저신뢰] — [렌트앤카](https://rentncar.com/product/%EC%9E%A5%EA%B8%B0%EB%A0%8C%ED%8A%B8%EC%B9%B4-%EA%B0%80%EA%B2%A9%ED%91%9C/20334/)

### Inferences
- **Break-even arithmetic** (illustrative, not data), at ₩38,500 per DB:
  - At a 1% lead→contract rate, DB cost is ₩3.85M per contract.
  - At 2%, it is ₩1.925M per contract.
  - At 3%, it is ₩1.28M per contract.
  - If per-contract commission is in the hundreds of thousands of won, profitability needs a lead→contract rate of roughly ≥3–5%, or a higher-margin mix (imports, corporate fleets).
  - Use this as a planning sensitivity, not a benchmark.
- The vendor's 9–13% "연결률" is far below 100%. Many purchased leads may never be reached, so the effective cost per *reached* lead could be ~₩300K+ if the figure is accurate.
- Because DB vendors often sell the same lead to several agents, the solo operator should negotiate written terms on 중복/결번/허위 교체 (replacement criteria and time window) before buying.

### Gaps
- No credible public conversion benchmarks (상담→견적→계약) from industry sources were found. Closed communities (네이버 카페, 오픈채팅, YouTube 영업 채널) were not reachable.
- No published per-DB price for auto rent/lease leads to compare with ₩38,500.
- Not researched in depth: segments (개인사업자, 법인, 사회초년생, 저신용/무심사, EV) and seasonality. The only related finding is that 무보증 products are harder to get approved with low credit — [겟차](https://web.getcha.kr/articles/rent-best-choice) [저신뢰]. Search tool limits also left this gap.

## 3. Legal and regulatory: advertising, 금소법, consumer data, marketing messages

### Takeaway
- **Lease ads by an intermediary** fall under 금소법 제22조. The agent needs a confirmation from the 직접판매업자 (capital company), must state that confirmation even on blogs and YouTube, and must include the mandatory disclosures.
- **Rent ads** sit outside 금소법 but remain subject to 표시광고법 (FTC). Examples include "월 OO만원" without the deposit, VAT or insurance assumptions, and "최저가" or "No.1" claims.
- **Lead collection** requires 개인정보보호법 consents, including separate 제3자 제공 consent to each 렌트사/캐피탈.
- **SMS/Kakao marketing** requires 정보통신망법 제50조 prior opt-in, the (광고) label, opt-out, and no sending from 21:00 to 08:00 without separate consent.
- **2026 amendment:** 정보통신망법 제50조의9 adds a penalty surcharge of up to 6% of revenue for illegal spam.

No FTC sanction or FSS consumer alert specifically against 장기렌트 false advertising was found.

### Cited Findings
- **금소법 advertising rules for intermediaries** (FSC Q&A, 2021-06-08):
  - Only listed parties may advertise financial products.
  - A 판매대리·중개업자 must obtain confirmation from every relevant 직접판매업자 and show "확인받음" on online media, including blogs and YouTube.
  - Pre-ad internal review is required.
  - Items under 제22조③1~3 cannot be omitted.
  - Benefits and risks must be presented in balance.
  - Hidden ads (뒷광고) are subject to the FTC 추천·보증 심사지침.
  - Portals and fintechs that actively intervene in sales count as advertisers and must register.
  — [금융위원회 금소법 FAQ](https://www.fsc.go.kr/no010101/76045)
- **Rental is not 금소법-regulated** (see the 서울경제 item in §1), so there is no 금소법 explanation duty for rent. — [서울경제](https://www.sedaily.com/article/14122016)
- **표시광고법 sanctions:** surcharge up to 2% of related revenue, or up to ₩5억 if revenue is hard to calculate. [secondary, law firm] — [대륜 공정거래](https://www.daeryunlaw-trade.com/lawInfo_new/9006). For the official law page, see [공정위 표시광고법](https://www.ftc.go.kr/www/selectCmitJrsdLawordList.do?key=302&searchLawordClCd=04).
- **Prior complaint about rental ads:** a consumer group challenged 롯데렌터카's "대한민국 No.1" and its terms and conditions, and threatened an FTC report. This is a complaint, not a sanction, and is about 3 years old. — [금융소비자뉴스](https://www.newsfc.co.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=61099)
- **Ad price vs real cost:** advertised "10만원대" rents often become ₩150–200K once deposit, VAT and insurance are included. This is a typical 표시광고 risk area. [저신뢰 blog] — [calarca.net](https://calarca.net/%EC%9E%A5%EA%B8%B0%EB%A0%8C%ED%8A%B8%EC%B9%B4-%EA%B4%91%EA%B3%A0%EC%99%80-%EC%8B%A4%EC%A0%9C-%EB%B9%84%EC%9A%A9-%EC%B0%A8%EC%9D%B4%EB%8A%94/)
- **FSS consumer alert, 2026-07-20:** fake "렌탈계약" (phone rental) used as collateral for illegal loans above 150% annual interest. The FSS says requests for 사업자등록 or 허위 렌탈계약 as a loan condition signal 불법사금융. This is not about car rent, but it is relevant to "무심사/저신용" ad messaging risk. — [아시아경제](https://view.asiae.co.kr/article/2026072011093483200)
- **정보통신망법 marketing messages:**
  - Prior consent is required for commercial ads.
  - Sending from 21:00 to 08:00 is prohibited without separate consent.
  - Violations bring fines or criminal penalties.

  — [법률신문](https://www.lawtimes.co.kr/opinion/197237)
- **2026 amendment (제50조의9):** a surcharge of up to 6% of revenue on illegal spam senders and negligent carriers, separate from fines. It takes effect 6 months after promulgation and depends on a presidential decree. Current effective status was **unconfirmed**. — [비즈고 블로그](https://blog.bizgo.io/inside/telecom-business-act-amendment-spam-penalty/)
- **Fine levels:** a blog cites fines of up to ₩30M for consent and labeling breaches. [저신뢰] — [clobe.ai](https://clobe.ai/blog/marketing-consent-optin-law-terms-guide)

### Inferences / [배경지식·미검증] (verify on law.go.kr / PIPC / KISA guidance)
- **[배경지식·미검증] 정보통신망법 제50조:**
  - Ad messages must start with "(광고)" and show the sender name and contact.
  - They must offer a free opt-out (e.g. 080 수신거부).
  - Senders must notify recipients of their consent status every 2 years (시행령 제62조의3).
  - An exception allows sending to customers who transacted within 6 months, for the same kind of goods.
  - The KISA 「불법스팸 방지 안내서」 is the practical reference.
  - The search did not confirm the 2-year rule.
- **[배경지식·미검증] 개인정보보호법 for lead forms:**
  - The lead form needs separate consents for (a) collection and use, listing items, purpose and retention period; (b) third-party provision to each named 렌트사/캐피탈 or "제휴 금융사 목록"; and (c) optional marketing consent.
  - Consents must not be bundled, and marketing consent cannot be required.
  - Credit checks (신용조회) by the 렌트사 need the consent of the 신용정보법 regime, which the company collects.
  - Buying DB from vendors requires that the vendor's original consent covered provision to the buyer. Otherwise the buyer's use may be unlawful (개인정보보호법 제17조, 제19조).
- **[배경지식·미검증] 여객자동차 운수사업법:** a 자동차대여사업 registration (렌터카 사업, including a minimum fleet requirement) is needed only for the company that owns and rents vehicles, not for a broker. Agents brokering on behalf of registered companies do not hold the license. Verify whether presenting oneself under a brand like "OO렌트카" could imply licensed status.
- **[배경지식·미검증] 여전법 / 여신금융협회:** lease advertising by capital companies goes through the association's 광고 심의. Agent-made lease ads need the 직접판매업자 confirmation above. In practice, capital companies often supply approved creatives.
- **Ad copy:** claims such as "무보증", "월 OO만원" and "최저가" carry 표시광고법 risk unless all conditions are shown nearby: 보증금/선납금, 기간, 약정주행거리, 차종·트림, VAT, 보험연령, and 잔존가치/인수가. This applies to rent and lease alike.

### Gaps
- No found FTC 의결 or FSS 보도자료 specifically against 장기렌트 agencies' false ads in 2025–2026.
- No authoritative source found for 여신금융협회 리스 광고 심의 rules (mandatory disclosure list for lease ads).
- Whether the 2026 정보통신망법 제50조의9 amendment is already in force was not confirmed.

## 4. Business setup: 사업자등록, 상호, 통신판매업

### Takeaway
No source in this session documented the correct 업종코드 for a 장기렌트 brokerage, or whether 통신판매업 신고 is needed. The points below are background knowledge to verify with 홈택스 or a 세무사.

### Cited Findings
- **Related tax point, customer-side:** for business customers, 70% of rent counts as a depreciation equivalent, capped at ₩8M per year. This is relevant to selling to 개인사업자 and 법인. — [carbay 2026](https://carbay.co.kr/board/blog/29) [저신뢰]

### Inferences / [배경지식·미검증]
- Brokers typically register as 서비스업 under 상품중개업 or 기타 사업지원 서비스업, and 리스 모집인 often as a 인적용역 (940909 기타모집수당). The correct code depends on whether income is paid as 사업소득 with 3.3% withholding (freelance 모집인) or as a VAT-able 세금계산서 commission (일반/간이과세자). Confirm on 홈택스 업종코드 조회.
- **통신판매업 신고:** generally required when selling goods or services to consumers online with remote contracting and payment. A broker who only collects leads, while the 렌트사 contracts and bills the customer, may arguably not be a 통신판매업자. Many operators register anyway (cheap, at the 구청). Unverified.
- **상호:** avoid names implying you are the rental company itself (e.g. "OO렌터카", "롯데" or "SK" brand terms). This avoids 상표 and 오인 issues and possible implications under 여객자동차법 and 표시광고법.

### Gaps
- No primary source confirmed the 업종코드 or the 통신판매업 obligation for 렌트 brokers.
