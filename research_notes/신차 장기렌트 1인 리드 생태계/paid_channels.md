# 신차 장기렌트 리드(DB) 유료 광고 채널 벤치마크 (한국, 2025–2026)

> Research date: 2026-10-13. About 17 search/fetch calls. Many primary pages (크몽, 아이보스, 커넥트리, 위픽부스터, db-store) returned HTTP 403, so some figures come only from search-result snippets. Those figures are marked **[snippet only]**. Most category-specific material comes from agencies or DB sellers that profit from the advice (mainly AMPM's inside.ampm.co.kr). **I found no independent, verified public CPC or CPL figures for 장기렌트 keywords.** Treat every number below as anecdotal unless it is marked otherwise.

## 1. 네이버 검색광고 (파워링크, 브랜드검색, 플레이스): CPC and strategy

### Takeaway
I found no published CPC for 장기렌트, 신차장기렌트, 장기렌트카, 자동차리스, or vehicle-model and region long-tail keywords. Every source says to pull figures from the 네이버 검색광고 키워드도구. Confirmed platform floors for 2026: 파워링크 from 70원 per click, 브랜드검색 from 1,000,000원 per 30 days, 플레이스 from 50원 per click. Agency guidance agrees on three tactics: vehicle-model plus 장기렌트 long-tail keywords, negative keywords such as 단기렌트 and 카셰어링, and separate ad groups by search intent.

### Cited Findings
- **2026 Naver ad minimums (as of 2026-09-08, VAT excluded):**
  - 파워링크: 70원 per click minimum. The source's practical budget is 300,000–1,000,000원 per month.
  - 브랜드검색: 1,000,000원 per 30 days minimum, on 7–90 day fixed-term contracts.
  - 플레이스광고: 50원 per click, capped at 5,000원.
  - 지역소상공인광고: 0.5원 per effective impression.
  - The source notes that competitive keywords can reach about 20,000–40,000원 per click at the top position. That figure comes from development and solution keywords, not car rental.
  - — [비즈팝콘, 2026-09](https://bizpopcorn.co.kr/blog/naver-ad-price)
- Power Link bids move like a market price: advertisers re-bid daily for top positions. For comparison, legal keywords run about 50,000–100,000원 per click (2022 press). — [전자신문 2022](https://www.etnews.com/20220729000137), [법률신문](https://www.lawtimes.co.kr/news/208902)
- **[snippet only]** An AMPM Power Link guide says vehicle-model plus main keyword (e.g. "BMW+장기렌트") **costs more per click than the main keyword but converts better.** It also gives this advice:
  - Exclude 단기렌트 and 카셰어링 as negative keywords.
  - Do not put 장기렌트, 자동차리스, and 법인차량 in one ad group; split them by intent.
  - — [AMPM insight 12973](https://inside.ampm.co.kr/insight/12973) (via search snippet; page not fetched)
- AMPM's general Power Link guide (2025-09-22) has no CPC figures. Its advice:
  - Use purchase-intent long-tail keywords.
  - Group keywords as main, sub, and region.
  - Give each ad group its own landing page.
  - Treat expensive head terms as a source of non-converting clicks.
  - — [AMPM insight 12625](https://inside.ampm.co.kr/insight/12625)

### Inferences
- **Break-even arithmetic, not a benchmark.** To beat a purchased DB at 38,500원, the maximum CPC is 38,500 × landing conversion rate:
  - At 3% conversion: about 1,155원 per click.
  - At 5% conversion: about 1,925원 per click.
  - At 10% conversion: about 3,850원 per click.
- Head terms such as 장기렌트 and 신차장기렌트 are contested by large comparison platforms and capital companies' agencies. Their top-position CPC is likely well above these break-even levels. This is unverified; the person should check it in the 키워드도구 (월간검색수, 평균클릭비용, 경쟁정도).
- Lower positions (4–10) and long-tail combinations look like the only realistic Power Link path for a 1–3M원 budget. Examples: 카니발 장기렌트 + 하이브리드, 쏘렌토 장기렌트 + 사업자, 지역 + 장기렌트.
- 브랜드검색 requires search demand for your own brand name. A new solo operator will have almost none, so it is not useful at the start.
- 플레이스 is cheap per click but depends on a physical-location listing. For a non-store remote sales operator, its fit is unclear.

### Gaps
- No published 키워드도구 CPC, search volume, or competition data for any 장기렌트 keyword. The person needs to query the tool directly, which requires an ad account.
- No figures for Power Link-to-lead conversion rates specific to car rental.

## 2. Meta (Instagram/Facebook) 잠재고객 양식: CPL, quality, creative, policy

### Takeaway
No public source I could reach gives an absolute KRW CPL for 장기렌트 Meta lead ads. Agencies agree on three points:
- CPL in this category is rising because of competition.
- Instant Forms produce many false or low-intent leads.
- Lower CPL often means lower contract rates.

The recommended setup is 2 ad sets, 4–5 creatives, broad targeting at the start, short forms, and fast call-back. Adding qualification questions or a CRM loop improves quality.

### Cited Findings
- AMPM (2025-11-27, part 1) describes the category as follows:
  - 장기렌트 is a category of same model and same terms, so differentiation is hard, and **CPL keeps rising with competition**.
  - Copy should address three customer anxieties: fake quotes ("허위견적 없습니다, 실제 출고가 기준"), slow consultation, and credit eligibility (저신용 / 무심사).
  - Monthly-saving claims are also used.
  - Use one vehicle and one benefit per creative.
  - Recommended form fields: 이름, 전화, 희망차종, 장기/리스/렌트 구분, 추가요청(optional). Keep required fields to a minimum.
  - A short form can roughly double conversion (no baseline given).
  - — [AMPM insight 13408](https://inside.ampm.co.kr/insight/13408)
- AMPM (2025-11-27, part 2):
  - Optimize the campaign for leads.
  - Ad set A: ages 25–50 with vehicle and interest targeting. Ad set B: broad (no targeting).
  - Use 4–5 creatives; more splits learning and raises CPL.
  - Broad targeting often beat narrow vehicle-interest targeting on CPL.
  - Claims initial CPL improves 30–50% with this structure (no absolute KRW figures).
  - — [AMPM insight 13409](https://inside.ampm.co.kr/insight/13409)
- AMPM (2026-08-31), "중고차/렌트/리스 업종, 허수 DB 걸러내는 광고운영 전략":
  - Aggressive low-price copy cuts CPA but brings in false leads. The example is "신형 그랜저 월 10만원대", which assumes 선납 50% and 60 months.
  - Add 2–3 dropdown steps to the form: 원하는 차종, 초기 예상 자금, 대략적인 신용점수 구간, 희망 출고일.
  - Feed conversion quality back to the Meta and Google algorithms.
  - No CPL figures given.
  - — [AMPM insight 60867](https://inside.ampm.co.kr/insight/60867)
- **[snippet only]**
  - AMPM content says Instant Form autofill generates low-intent submissions. Suggested fixes are qualification questions, a "higher intent" form, and CRM integration.
  - One automotive case claims valid-DB share rose **from 32% to 60%** after CRM integration.
  - Related posts are titled "DB는 늘었는데 계약은 왜 줄었을까" and "DB가 많이 들어온다고 좋은 광고일까요".
  - — [AMPM video 4485](https://inside.ampm.co.kr/video/4485), [AMPM 59768](https://inside.ampm.co.kr/insight/59768), [AMPM 59763](https://inside.ampm.co.kr/insight/59763)
- **Special Ad Category / financial:** I found no authoritative source confirming whether Meta's "Financial products and services" Special Ad Category is enforced for Korean advertisers or applies to 장기렌트.
  - A marketing blog claims Meta expanded advertiser identity verification to financial categories in 2025–2026. This is not confirmed by Meta.
  - — [calywire, Meta ads policy update May 2026](https://calywire.com/meta-ads-policy-update-may-2026/) (unverified)
  - In Korea, loan-brokering ads in practice require 대부중개업 registration plus required disclosures. That applies to loans, not rental.
  - — [AMPM 광고 제한 사항](https://inside.ampm.co.kr/insight/6721)

### Inferences
- In other markets, Special Ad Category rules for credit or finance remove age, gender, and zip-code targeting. It is plausible but unverified that Korean car-lease or rental ads mentioning 저신용 or 무심사 could be flagged under those rules or Meta's general financial-services policy. Copy promising 무심사 or 저신용 OK is the highest-risk wording.
- Because agencies run broad targeting anyway, losing detailed targeting would hurt less than in other categories.
- A plausible minimum for one learning-capable campaign (2 ad sets × daily budget) is about 1M원 per month. At 38,500원 CPL, 1M원 buys only about 26 leads. Meta's learning phase typically needs about 50 conversions per ad set per week, so a 1–3M원 budget will probably never fully exit learning. This is an inference.
- Creative types such as 릴스 견적 비교 and 차량 출고 영상 are not documented with performance data in any source found. Agencies only stress "one car + one benefit" and anxiety-relieving copy.

### Gaps
- No absolute KRW CPL for 장기렌트 Meta lead ads in 2025–2026 from any reachable source. Anecdotal figures probably exist in closed communities (영업인 카페, 오픈채팅) but could not be accessed.
- No official Meta Korea confirmation on Special Ad Category scope.

## 3. 기타 채널 (카카오모먼트/비즈보드, 카카오톡 채널, 당근, 토스, 유튜브, 네이버 GFA, 클립)

### Takeaway

## 4. 장기렌트 DB 구매 시장: 단가, 품질, 허위/중복

### Takeaway
Listed CPA prices for new-car 장기렌트 DB cluster around **38,000–42,500원 per lead (VAT included)**, with a 50-lead minimum and invalid-DB replacement terms. That is consistent with the person's 38,500원 benchmark. Low-credit (저신용) DB is priced separately. Sellers' "중복/허수 최소화" claims are marketing, and no independent duplicate or false-lead rate was found.

### Cited Findings

  - 38,000원 or 42,500원 per DB (VAT included), depending on ad channel.
  - Minimum contract of 50 leads.
  - Unit price may change with invalid-DB conditions and channel conditions.

- A first-time solo advertiser without learned pixels or creative history will likely see a higher CPL at first.

## 5. 랜딩/폼 전환율 및 품질 필터 필드

### Takeaway
I found no numeric landing-page or form conversion benchmark specific to 장기렌트. Agencies offer two competing prescriptions:
- Minimal required fields for volume (이름, 전화, 차종, 상품유형).
- 2–3 dropdown qualifiers to filter false leads (차종, 초기자금, 신용 구간, 희망 출고일).

### Cited Findings
- Minimal-field approach: required fields should be minimal for stable CPL and conversion, and the counselor collects the rest by phone. Shortening the form can double conversion (no baseline given). — [AMPM 13408](https://inside.ampm.co.kr/insight/13408)
- Qualifying-field approach: add 원하는 차종, 초기 예상 자금, 신용점수 구간, 희망 출고일 as dropdown steps to filter 허수. — [AMPM 60867](https://inside.ampm.co.kr/insight/60867)
- **[snippet only]** Valid-DB share went from 32% to 60% with CRM integration (automotive case). — [AMPM video 4485](https://inside.ampm.co.kr/video/4485)

### Inferences
- 사업자 여부 (개인/개인사업자/법인) is a strong qualifier because business buyers favor 장기렌트 for expense treatment. No source documented its effect.
- A practical compromise is one or two dropdowns (차종 and 출고 희망 시기) plus a credit-band question. This trades some CPL for higher contactability.

### Gaps
- No landing conversion rate (%) benchmarks for 장기렌트 in 2025–2026 were found.

## 6. 첫 광고주가 실패하는 이유 (경쟁 구조)

### Takeaway
Sources consistently describe the category as hyper-competitive, with the same products sold at the same terms. That pushes CPL upward and incentivizes deceptive low-price hooks, which then produce low-quality leads. No source quantified first-timer failure rates or comparison platforms' ad spend.

### Cited Findings
- The category is a "동일 모델·동일 조건 경쟁" market, differentiation is hard, and CPL keeps rising. — [AMPM 13408](https://inside.ampm.co.kr/insight/13408)
- Competitors chase low CPA with provocative copy such as price-emphasis hooks with hidden 선납금, which increases false DB. — [AMPM 60867](https://inside.ampm.co.kr/insight/60867); [아이보스 seller post](https://www.i-boss.co.kr/ab-2987-527704) [snippet only]
- Too many ad sets or creatives, or narrow targeting at the start, prevent Meta learning and spike costs. These are common beginner errors. — [AMPM 13409](https://inside.ampm.co.kr/insight/13409)
- Power Link head terms draw clicks without purchase intent. Negative keywords and intent grouping are needed. — [AMPM 12625](https://inside.ampm.co.kr/insight/12625); [AMPM 12973 snippet](https://inside.ampm.co.kr/insight/12973)
- Well-funded comparison platforms and apps compete for the same demand: 다나와자동차, 차픽, 공카, and others. — [다나와 자동차](https://auto.danawa.com/leaserent/), [차픽](https://apps.apple.com/kr/app/id1603395190)

### Inferences
- With a 1–3M원 budget, a solo operator gets about 26–78 leads per month at 38,500원 equivalence. That is below the volume Meta needs to optimize reliably and too little to test many keywords.
- Likely failure modes:
  - Spreading the budget across too many channels.
  - Bidding on head terms.
  - Low-price hook copy that inflates false leads.
  - Slow call-back.
  - Judging success by CPL instead of contract cost.
- A reasonable test design is 1–2 channels only: Meta lead form plus long-tail Power Link. Keep buying DB as a baseline and compare cost per contract after 4–8 weeks.

### Gaps
- No data on 카디랩, 겟차, or 모두렌트 ad spend. These platforms were not found in reachable sources, and 카디랩's existence or name could not be verified.
- No survey or statistics on first-time advertiser failure in this category.
